Getting started

What does TreeStyle do?

Snap a photo of your Christmas tree and TreeStyle looks at it with a designer's eye. You get a style score, a step-by-step plan, a shopping list and a preview of your finished tree.

What's the difference between Check my tree and Design my tree?

Check my tree works with what's already on your tree and suggests the finishing touches that lift it. Design my tree styles a tree from the start: your bare tree, the room it'll go in, or an idea you describe.

What makes a good photo?

Stand back so the whole tree is in view, top included. Shoot straight on, at about the middle of the tree, with the lights on and the room lights soft. A real photo works best, not a screenshot.

Does it work for real trees?

Yes. Real trees skip Shape the branches, and we design with their natural gaps rather than filling them.

Your design

How does the style score work?

We look at the tree the way a designer would: ribbon, fullness, balance, variety and the finishing touches, then give it a score out of 5. It's strict on purpose, so a 5 means designer-level work.

Is the preview a real photo?

No. The preview is an AI picture of your tree with the plan applied, so you can see where it's heading. Real pieces will vary a little in color and size.

Can I change my design?

Yes. Tap Change something on the preview to adjust colors, pieces or ribbon, or try another style. Undo takes you back a step.

What if my tree is already decorated?

That's what Check my tree is for. We keep what's working and only suggest what will make the biggest difference.

Shopping

Where do the shopping links go?

Most go to Amazon, either to a piece our favorite designers love or to a search for exactly what your plan calls for. You can buy the same pieces at Michaels, Hobby Lobby or anywhere you like.

Does TreeStyle earn from purchases?

Draft As an Amazon Associate, TreeStyle earns from qualifying purchases. It never changes what you pay.

Why don't the product pictures match exactly?

Our pictures show the color and finish of each piece, not the exact product. Tap Shop to see the product itself.

How much will it cost?

Draft It depends on your tree's size and how much you already own. The shopping list gives amounts, so you can buy only what you need.

Sharing and privacy

Where are my trees saved?

Draft For now, your trees are saved on this phone. Once accounts arrive, they'll be saved to your account too.

How do I share my tree?

On Your finished tree, tap Share to post a branded picture to Instagram, Facebook or Pinterest, or Download to keep a high-resolution copy.

What happens when I share to the gallery?

We look over every tree before it appears in Trees our community styled. Only your first name, your photo and your review are shown.

Can I remove a review I shared?

Draft Yes. Write to us and we'll take it down.
