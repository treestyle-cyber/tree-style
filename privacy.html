Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

TreeStyle ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you join the TreeStyle waitlist (on treestyle.net or through a sign-up form on Facebook or Instagram) or use the TreeStyle app, and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your first name and email address. If you sign up through a Facebook or Instagram form, Meta shares those details with us.

How we use it

  • To let you know when TreeStyle opens and to send early access.
  • To send occasional TreeStyle news, styling tips and offers.

We do not sell your information.

Using the TreeStyle app

When you use the app, you sign in with your email address. We keep your tree photo, your answers about your tree, and the designs, plans and previews made for it, together with your email, so we can fix problems and improve TreeStyle. We keep them until January 31, 2027, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. We do not post your photos anywhere unless you choose to share your tree in the TreeStyle gallery.

To design your tree, your photo and answers are sent to the AI services that power TreeStyle (Anthropic and Google), which process them to create your plan and preview. Payments for the Holiday Pass are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.

Who helps us

We use Mailchimp to store our waitlist and send email, Clerk for app sign-in, Stripe for payments and Vercel to host the app and store your designs. When you sign up through Facebook or Instagram, Meta processes the form under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us at any time to ask us to update or delete your information.
