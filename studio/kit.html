Brand kit

The basic look of everything at once: colors, fonts, buttons, background, icons and logos, used on every screen of the app and the website. Pictures for particular spots live under Photos. Changes show in the preview on the right as you make them; nothing changes for anyone else until you tap Save brand kit.

The overall style of the app. Pick one as a starting point, then adjust anything below. The website keeps its own look.

Colors

Fonts

Five roles, the same on the app and the website. For each, choose the font, weight, size, letter spacing, capitals, color (on the page, and separately inside cards), italic and alignment. Anything you leave alone stays as designed. Subheadlines follow the headline font, and labels and buttons follow the body font, unless you give them their own. Upload your own font file (WOFF2, WOFF, OTF or TTF) once it is licensed.

Buttons

The shape of every button. Their lettering is under Fonts → Buttons.

Background

The page behind every screen of the app and the website.

Navigation bar

A cream bar along the bottom of the app (Home · Style · Saved · Shop · Profile) with a "Shop the Look" banner above it on the home screen, from Kylie's design. It is off until Eric approves; turning it on here shows it in the preview, and saving turns it on for this copy of the app. Studio → Screens always shows it in its own section.

Icons

The tab bar at the bottom of the app. Upload an SVG line drawing for each; any color is fine, because the app colors it (Midnight when selected, Walnut when not, from Colors). Draw them on the same square canvas with the same line weight so they read as one set.

Logos

Replace a logo everywhere it appears. A transparent PNG or SVG works best.
