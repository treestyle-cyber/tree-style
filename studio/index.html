Each slot is a photo the app and site use. Generate, adjust the wording if you like, and tap Use this on the one you love. It appears in the app right away. Until you choose one, that spot keeps its fabric texture.

One step first: photos you choose are saved in Vercel Blob storage, which isn't connected yet. In Vercel, open the tree-style project → Storage → Create → Blob → connect it to this project → then Redeploy. You can still generate and preview images in the meantime.
